Q1. WHAT SPECIFICALLY ACCOUNTS FOR THE REMAINING 1.06 -> 0.35 RESIDUAL/MATCHED GAP?

  IT IS NOT A RESIDUAL-CONTROL PROBLEM. It is a MATCHED-TURNOVER problem. Five things are ruled out
  by measurement, and one survives:

  RULED OUT -- we are not reluctant to complete. State-normalised at equal run length, arm 3 flips
    to the opposite side MORE often than L5 does, at every run length we can observe:
       run 1  52.4% vs 37.5% | run 2  43.2% vs 35.8% | run 3  72.2% vs 31.0% | pooled 52.6% vs 31.2%
    Our mean same-side run is 1.59; L5's is 3.03. Our completion discipline is already more eager
    than the trader we are copying. There is no patience defect left to fix.

  RULED OUT -- liquidity. MARKET_LIMIT is 3 of 97 residual units.
  RULED OUT -- missing complements. ORDER_ABSENT is 2 of 97. The "rest both sides" fix of 5a13f0b0 holds.
  RULED OUT -- the 0.98 cap, now that it is gone. PRICE_POLICY_LIMIT collapsed from 46.0% of units
    (58 of 126, and 74% of the dollars) to 14.4% (14 of 97). Of those 14, twelve were reachable only
    at the MARKETABLE price, i.e. only by crossing -- which is prohibited without separate authorisation.
  MOSTLY NOT A DEFECT -- WINDOW_CENSORING is the largest single bucket at 37.1%, but 33 of its 36 units
    are markets that have simply not expired yet in a live cohort. Only 3 units are real short-window loss.

  WHAT SURVIVES -- THE DENOMINATOR. L5 does not carry a smaller residual buffer than we do. It carries a
    similar buffer and cycles far more matched inventory past it inside one market:

                                            L5            PMUS arm 3      ratio
       fills per ACTIVE minute (p50)       3.000            0.429          7.0x
       inter-fill gap (p50)                   2s             127s           63x
       recycle latency, fill -> opposite     10s             217s           22x
       price drift over that latency      0.0300           0.0600          2.0x
       acquisitions per market (p50)            6                2
       |residual| growth across episode      3.1x             1.2x
       MATCHED growth across episode        32.8x             6.0x

Q1b. A SECOND FIDELITY DISCREPANCY SURFACED, AND IT IS THE SAME ONE SEEN TWICE

  The cap-versus-no-cap dilemma is an artefact of the slow clock, not a real choice:

       arm 2 (cap 0.98)   basis p50 0.9350   edge/unit +0.1155   r/m 2.150   standalone -25.23%
       arm 3 (L5 rule)    basis p50 1.0000   edge/unit +0.0040   r/m 0.889   standalone -13.71%
       L5                 basis      0.9708  edge/unit +0.0292   r/m 0.347   standalone  -0.14%

  The cap buys basis and sells geometry. Removing it buys geometry and sells basis. L5 held BOTH,
  because its completion landed 10 seconds and 1 cent after the acquisition -- at that distance the
  touch still IS a 0.97 basis. Ours lands 217 seconds and 6 cents later, by which time the touch is par.
  Basis and geometry are not two problems. They are one: the drift between an acquisition and its completion.

  AND IT IS NOT THE SETTLEMENT CLOCK. bb6ae542 stands. L5's own lanes settle it: the 15-minute BTC lane
  has the BEST geometry (r/m 0.187) and the BEST basis (0.9666) while the 5-minute BTC lane is six times
  worse (1.115). Window length does not order the geometry; inter-fill drift does, monotonically across
  the three lanes carrying real size. Selecting PMUS markets on time-to-settlement was never going to
  work, and did not.

Q2. WOULD ELIMINATING THAT GAP BRING PMUS CLOSE ENOUGH TO BREAKEVEN FOR INCENTIVES TO MATTER?

  GEOMETRY ALONE: NO, AND NOT CLOSE. -13.71% -> -5.94%. It would need 594 bps of incentive. PMUS pays
    a measured 97.59 bps. Six times short.
  TOXICITY ALONE: NO. -5.89%, needs 589 bps.
  GEOMETRY + TOXICITY: NO. -2.43%, needs 243 bps. Still 2.5x more than PMUS pays.
  GEOMETRY + TOXICITY + MATCHED BASIS: YES, and only here. -0.14%, needs 14 bps, and the measured
    97.59 bps covers it roughly seven times over (+0.83% of capital with the rebate credited).

  So the answer is conditional and the condition is specific: the matched basis is the term that decides
  it. It carries 2.29 of the 13.57 points of the total gap but it is the only one whose removal drops the
  required incentive rate below what this venue actually pays. Fixing residual geometry without fixing
  the basis leaves us needing a 6x richer incentive environment than the one we can document.

  For scale, L5's own incentive intensity was +$68,208 against $728,500 of spend = 936 bps, roughly ten
  times the PMUS rate. We do not need ten times. We need fourteen basis points -- but only from a state
  we have not yet reached on any arm.
