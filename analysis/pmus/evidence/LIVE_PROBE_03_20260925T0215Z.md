## What happened, action by action

29 L5 actions, and every one is accounted for: **13 + 1 + 15 = 29**.

1. **13 refused `STRICT_NOT_EVALUABLE`** at PMUS_TRANSLATION — L5 resting
   strictly inside the spread with no observed interaction, which the frozen
   rule refuses rather than guess. Ordinary, and the same refusal the
   recorder logs every window.
2. **1 reached `submit()`** — translated, passed the safety overlay, had its
   envelope reserved before the wire, and then `transport.submit()` raised.
   Not `NotArmed`: a real exception, on the armed path. The code does the one
   correct thing —

       self.store.mark_uncertain("submit raised %s for %s" % ...)
       prov.stage("ORDER", {"submitted": None,
                            "reason": "SUBMIT_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN", ...})

   `submitted: None`, not False. It does **not** release the reservation,
   because an order that may have reached the venue is still consuming the
   envelope.
3. **15 refused `ORDER_STATE_UNCERTAIN`** — every action after that one, cut
   off at `SafetyLimits.refuse()` before any dollar check. The session
   refused to send anything further until the venue answered.

That ordering is forced by the code: `ORDER_STATE_UNCERTAIN` can only be
returned once `mark_uncertain` has been called, and only `SUBMIT_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`
calls it here. So the single attempt was the first, and nothing followed it.

## What is not known, and cannot be from here

The **exception type and message** behind `SUBMIT_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`. It is
journaled per action as `ORDER.detail` to
`/state/pmus-ws/l5_session_journal.jsonl` on the probe's volume, which cannot
be read without a running container. The stdout report carries only the
reason counts.

`PmusTransport.submit()` does preview -> exact-echo validation -> POST, so
the candidates are: the preview call failing; the echo gate REFUSING a
venue-normalised price (the 14:40Z behaviour that made `require_echo` a
refusal rather than a reprice); or the order POST itself erroring or timing
out. **These are very different events** — the first two mean nothing
order-creating was ever sent and the uncertainty was conservative; the third
means the uncertainty was real. The venue reconciliation resolves the
*outcome* either way, but not the *cause*, and the cause decides whether
anything needs fixing.
