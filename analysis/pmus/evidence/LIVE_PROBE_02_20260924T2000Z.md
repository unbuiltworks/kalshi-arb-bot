subscribes to the named market through the recorder's own frame builder and

## What this does NOT settle, and the open item

The probe exited at **20:03:50Z**, roughly 9.5 minutes before its
`NO_NEW_ORDERS_AFTER_S` cutoff at 20:13:30Z, having handled **zero** L5
actions. Over the same window the recorder observed 110-134 L5 actions per
window with a healthy feed.

`frames: 4` and `book_states: 1` are also far below what the recorder sees
on the same stream (thousands of book states per window).

Nothing in stdout explains the early exit. The runner prints one line, at
the end; the intermediate `probe_error` record is journalled to
`/state/pmus-ws/l5_live_probe_journal.jsonl` on the probe's volume, which
is not readable from outside the container. An exception raised inside
`work_window` would be caught by the outer handler, journalled, and would
jump straight to close-out — which is consistent with a clean
reconciliation, a consumed authorization, and an otherwise healthy report.

So: the WS connected and the probe armed, but the probe did not work its
window, and the reason is not yet in evidence. That is the next question,
and it is a diagnosis question, not a money-safety one — exposure was $0
throughout and every close-out step ran.
