## Why the probe stopped

**Registered clean-stop criterion 7 fired outright.** Action retention 0.679
against a floor of 0.75; contract retention 0.592 against 0.70. Both floors
were predeclared from the OOS replay, which predicted 0.841 and 0.785. Live
suppression was **32%** where the replay said 15.9% — twice as harsh.

The mechanism is visible in the bind table: L5 delivers same-side actions in
**bursts** (five at 11:41:58, seven at 11:43:32). Once the residual is at the
cap, every member of a burst is refused, because nothing repairs between them.
The replay, walking actions one at a time against a residual that moved after
each, never saw that clustering. This is a real and material difference
between replay and live, and it is the probe's main finding.

**Registered hard-stop criterion 1 was VIOLATED (confirmed after the probe), and
the invariant it asserts is not one the implementation provides.** The cap reads *realized*
residual (`inventory_class`: "Realized, not realized-plus-outstanding") and
does not count the up-to-8 resting orders. So same-side orders resting when
the gate last measured can fill afterwards and carry `|residual|` past
`k × clip`. The gate bounds **the decision**, not **the inventory**.

**RESOLVED 2026-09-27, after the probe, by rebuilding the order tape from the
captured `L5_LIVE_LIFECYCLE` stream: the invariant WAS violated. Peak realized
`|residual|` reached 3.24 against a cap of 1.80 — an 80% overshoot.**

## What this probe does and does not establish

**Establishes.** The gate is wired, fires live on both sides, and refuses only
actions the frozen path would have sent. Settlement reconciles to authoritative
cash exactly, twice, with zero unattributed difference. Fees and credited
rebates are both confirmed zero. Matched pair cost came in at 0.95854, inside
the written spring rule.

**Does not establish.** Nothing about profitability. Two markets, one of them
truncated and binding zero times, is not evidence of expectancy in either
direction, and the +$0.903100 is a sample. It also does not establish that the
cap bounds inventory — the evidence points the other way.
