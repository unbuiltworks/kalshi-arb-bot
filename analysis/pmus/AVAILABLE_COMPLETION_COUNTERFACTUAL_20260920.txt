units ALSO ACQUIRES R more legs, so the counterfactual denominator must grow too:

    completion_cf = 2*(matched + extra) / (acquired_legs + extra)

My first pass omitted that and produced 106% completion, which is impossible.
Fixed below; the corrected figures are lower and are the ones reported.
