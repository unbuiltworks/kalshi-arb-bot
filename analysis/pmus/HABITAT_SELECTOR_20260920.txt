WHY THIS IS THE RIGHT TARGET. 81f77fea proved R/M = 2I/(1-I) EXACTLY, so the habitat
is one number: the imbalance of flow a resting two-sided maker can actually be reached
by. Direction is not carried by the PMUS tape, but it IS supportable against the
prevailing book, because a resting maker is reachable from one side at a time:

    print px <= bid   seller hit the bid    -> reaches a resting BUY YES
    print px >= ask   buyer lifted the ask  -> reaches a resting BUY NO
    bid < px < ask    UNSUPPORTED -> excluded, and its share is carried as a feature
                      so the exclusion is never silent (measured mean 2.6%)

No P&L, no settlement, none of our own fills and nothing after the decision instant
enters any feature. Two regression tests assert exactly that.

Every apparently L5-balanced PMUS market is a small sample that came out even. THE
