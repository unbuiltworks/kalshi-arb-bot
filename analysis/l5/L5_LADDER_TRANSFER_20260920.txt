  "If multi-rung mechanics improve held-out L5 fidelity AND materially close PMUS geometry, continue."
     Held-out L5 fidelity: YES, decisively -- distance 1.229 -> 0.410, monotone in R, generalises
     TRAIN->OOS with no refit. The ladder is L5. We have earned the right to call it that.
     PMUS geometry: PARTIALLY -- cohort r/m 1.09 -> 0.92, completion 64.7% -> 68.5%, and the
     return improves from -13.71% to -8.68%, the best of any arm built so far.

  "If they improve L5 fidelity but don't improve PMUS, we have evidence of a genuine
   transfer/microstructure limitation."
     This is where we land, and the limitation is now PROVEN, not inferred:

       R/M = 2I/(1-I) exactly, where I is the imbalance of acquisitions across the two sides.
       Every arm sits ON its own floor to three decimals. Our execution already captures
       100% of the pairing its markets offer. The ZERO-QUEUE 20-rung +/-10-tick upper bound
       has the SAME imbalance (0.327) as the frozen 4-rung arm (0.315) -- reach, rung count
       and queue position move both sides together and cannot change their balance.

       L5's habitat   I = 0.083  ->  r/m floor 0.182  (achieved 0.182)
       PMUS habitat   I = 0.315  ->  r/m floor 0.919  (achieved 0.919)

     STOP CONDITION B IS MET. The residual 0.92 -> 0.182 gap is structurally impossible on
     these PMUS markets. It is not a fidelity defect we have left unimplemented.

  "Do not call the strategy profitable unless the faithful prospective version is positive
   after actual executable economics."
     IT IS NOT. The most faithful implementation we can build returns -8.68% standalone,
     -13.24% under the pessimistic queue bound. Attributable incentives are $0.00 confirmed.
     Break-even needs 868-1324 bps against the 97.59 bps this venue has actually been
     observed to pay -- short by 9x to 14x. No incentive assumption available to us bridges
     that, and I am not going to manufacture one.

  "If faithful replay gets standalone close to breakeven, immediately build the same frozen
   implementation into prospective shadow."
     PRECONDITION NOT MET. -8.68% is not close to breakeven, so prospective shadow is NOT

WHAT THIS BUYS US, AND THE ONE QUESTION IT OPENS
========================================================================================================================
The transfer programme is not dead; its target is. We now have an evidence-derived,
single-number habitat criterion that did not exist before: ACQUISITION FLOW IMBALANCE I.
It is measurable before committing capital, it predicts r/m exactly rather than
statistically, and it explains every arm we have built including the failures.

L5 did not have better execution than us. It had I = 0.083. We are working markets at
I = 0.315. Sports and esports books are directional -- the flow arrives on the side the
crowd wants, and a two-sided maker cannot pair what never comes.

THE OPEN QUESTION, stated but deliberately NOT acted on here: PMUS per-market I has p10
of 0.029, so a minority of PMUS markets ARE L5-balanced. Selecting on I computed from our
own realised acquisitions would be fitting a selector to an outcome-correlated variable on
exploratory data, which is exactly what we were told not to do. If this is worth pursuing,
it needs I estimated from PRE-ENTRY book/flow evidence, preregistered, and tested on a
fresh prospective cohort. That is a new experiment, not a continuation of this one.
