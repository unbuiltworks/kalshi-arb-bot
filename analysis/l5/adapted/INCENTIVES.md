**Ten rows across the entire 78,000-row capture.** Not an estimate: these are
credited rows in L5's own public activity.

Three days carry all three streams. Mean over those days: **≈\$3,436/day.**

## Every one of these is ACCOUNT-LEVEL

**All ten rows have an empty `eventSlug`. Zero are attributable to BTC15m, or
to any market.** L5 trades BTC5m, ETH5m, ETH15m and more, so these credits
cover his whole book.

**No allocation to BTC15m is made here, and none should be made.** The question
"what share of the \$3,436/day did BTC15m earn?" cannot be answered from this
record.

## What can be said, and what cannot

**Can be said.** On the three days with complete coverage, account-level
incentives ran ≈\$3,436/day while BTC15m trading economics over 527 windows
totalled **−\$151**. Incentives are larger than the trading result by orders of
magnitude, and larger than the \$480/day program target by roughly 7×.

**Also can be said, and it is the striking part.** The incentive regime
*changed*. One maker-rebate row in the whole of March–April (\$29.94 on
04-21) against ~\$1,300/day of maker rebate in late August. And L5's BTC15m
trading P&L flips sign across exactly that boundary: **+\$429.53 in spring
(ret/\$ +0.0153), −\$580.40 in August (ret/\$ −0.00115)**.

He was trading-profitable when incentives were absent, and trading-negative
once they were large.

**Cannot be said.** That the incentives *caused* the shift, that BTC15m earned
any particular share, or that incentives are his objective. Three days of
incentive data against a six-month trading record is thin, the credits are
unattributable by market, and a coincidence of timing is not a mechanism.

**The hypothesis is consistent with the evidence and is not established.** It
is recorded as a hypothesis.

## What would settle it

Per-market or per-fill incentive attribution, which this venue does not publish
for another trader's account. Failing that, a longer run of daily account-level
credits spanning both regimes would at least establish whether the shift in
trading P&L tracks the incentive schedule or merely coincides with it once.

## Discipline

Incentives are excluded from every qualification economic in the ADAPTED
lineage, and are never netted into trading P&L anywhere.
