# `rejected_at_non_crossing_price` — execution-adapter investigation

Read-only. **No production, risk, cap, band, retry, shell, admission or
execution-mode change was made.** The shadow candidate in §6 is NOT
implemented.

## 1. VERDICT

**The venue was RIGHT on every rejection. This is not a venue defect and not a
pricing-math defect. It is a STALE-BOOK RACE, plus a self-diagnosis label that
is generated by the stalest source we have.**

The `implementation bug candidate:` prefix is misleading and is itself the
finding: it fires precisely when the REST gateway BBO — the least fresh input
in the path — says we were not crossing.

## 3. THE CHECKLIST, ITEM BY ITEM

**Venue-authored rejection reason — NONE EXISTS.** The executor searches every
key containing reason/reject/error/text/message/code and falls back to
`fields=` only when nothing is found; the fallback is what fired. The venue's
24-key order record carries no reason field at all. **The venue does not tell
us why.** That is a venue API limitation, recorded as such.

**Price normalization / tick rounding — CORRECT.** Engine px and venue price
are exact complements: engine 0.52 -> venue 0.48, and on the filled control
`CG27K3EGEVB2` engine 0.56 -> venue 0.44 with `commissionSpreadPx 0.56`
confirming the frame. Tick 0.01, `declared_by_venue`. No rounding artifact:
every price is an exact cent.

**Side mapping — CORRECT.** `opp_ask_authenticated = 1 - yes_bid` verified at
both snapshots: yes_bid 0.43 -> 0.57, and yes_bid 0.50 -> 0.50.

**Order payload — WELL FORMED.** Nothing malformed, no missing quantity, no
bad TIF.

**Market state — OPEN.** `MARKET_STATUS_OPEN`, `active true`, `closed false`,
`ep3Status OPEN`, `infra_rejections 0`. Not a halt, not maintenance.

**Post-only semantics — CANNOT BE CONFIRMED FROM THE ECHO.** The venue's
returned order record has no `postOnly` / `execInst` field among its 24 keys,
so the flag cannot be verified round-trip. The observed behaviour is
*consistent* with post-only-rejects-on-cross, but the echo does not prove the
flag was transmitted. **UNKNOWN, and reported as UNKNOWN.**

**Stale-book timing — THIS IS THE CAUSE.**

## 4. THE RACE, WITH TIMESTAMPS

The authenticated WS tape moved while we were pricing:

```
12:06:36.961   yes_bid 0.43   yes_ask 0.48    opp_ask_authenticated 0.57
12:06:47.047   yes_bid 0.50   yes_ask 0.52    opp_ask_authenticated 0.50
```

The YES bid rose **7 ticks in ~10 seconds**. Our order is, in venue terms, a
SELL of YES at 0.48. Against yes_bid 0.43 it does not cross. Against yes_bid
0.50 **it crosses by 2 ticks** and post-only must reject it.

Book age at each order's `createTime`:

| order | book snapshot used | createTime | **book age** |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CG2CP77R0VB7` | 12:06:36.961 | 12:06:43.630 | **6.67 s** |
| `CG2BV93SYVB8` | 12:06:36.961 | 12:06:45.031 | **8.07 s** |
| `CG2D1DENAVB7` | 12:06:47.047 | 12:06:48.654 | 1.61 s |

Orders 1 and 2 were priced from a book **6.7 and 8.1 seconds old**, against a
`max_quote_age_s` of 5.0 that nothing enforces at submit time.

## 5. AND THE "5 TICKS" NUMBER IS FROM THE WORST SOURCE

`rejection_margin_ticks` is computed in `_classify_rejection` as
`(opp_ask - px) / tick` where `opp_ask` comes from `q1.outcome_bbo` — the
**REST gateway**, fetched AFTER the rejection.

**That REST value read 0.5700 on all three rejections, spanning 12:06:44 to
12:06:49 — while the authenticated WS tape at 12:06:47 read 0.50.** The REST
gateway was stale by **7 ticks** relative to the authenticated book, for at
least five seconds.

So: **no, our "5 ticks non-crossing" calculation did NOT use the book the venue
evaluated against.** It used a post-hoc REST snapshot that was demonstrably
wrong. The engine's own `;authenticated_book_crossed` cross-check caught the
disagreement — but only on the third order, because that was the only one whose
WS snapshot happened to be fresh (0.3 s). On orders 1 and 2 the WS snapshot was
itself the stale 12:06:36 one, so both sources agreed at 0.57 and the
disagreement was invisible.

`disagrees_with_gateway: true` is set on `CG2D1DENAVB7` and null on the others.

## 6. SHADOW CANDIDATE — SMALLEST FORM, NOT IMPLEMENTED

Scope: **this rejection class only.** Nothing else changes.

> On a rejection whose class contains `rejected_at_non_crossing_price`:
> 1. obtain a **fresh authenticated book** (WS tape, with its age asserted
>    against `max_quote_age_s`, not the REST gateway);
> 2. **recompute the legal maker price from scratch** from that book;
> 3. permit a further submission **only if** the refreshed state **materially
>    differs** (recomputed price differs, or the book moved by >= 1 tick) AND
>    every existing ceiling / risk / post-only / band / cap gate passes
>    unchanged;
> 4. otherwise **do not resubmit** — yield the attempt.
>
> **Never resend an identical order against an unchanged book.**

What it must NOT touch, and does not: price policy, pair ceiling, size, band,
cap, admission, concurrency, settlement horizon, post-only status, maker-only
status, campaign deadline, retry cadence for any other class, or any risk
breaker. Every risk/safety condition still cancels immediately.

This is **not** CEILING_PINNED_PERSIST, which stays PARKED. That one suppresses
a `touch_moved` cancel; this one adds a freshness precondition after a specific
rejection.

**Evidence it does not have:** 4 rejections, 1 market, two ~2 s windows. It
would have prevented orders 2 and 3 (book unchanged/stale, identical price) but
not order 1. No fill-conversion improvement may be attributed to it until
filled/settled evidence demonstrates one.

## 7. SEPARATE OBSERVABILITY DEFECT — 3x DUPLICATE EMISSION

Every rejected order is emitted **three times** on the `PMUS_ORDER_REJECTED`
line: 12 log rows for 4 distinct rejections. Two of the three land within
~50 ms (one pair 5 MICROSECONDS apart: 12:06:44.640215690 and
.640220651), the third seconds to a minute later.

```
CG2CP77R0VB7   12:06:44.640, 12:06:44.640, 12:06:51.183
CG2BV93SYVB8   12:06:46.072, 12:06:46.127, 12:06:51.137
CG2D1DENAVB7   12:06:49.131, 12:06:49.202, 12:06:51.253
CG2J11BPEVB7   12:15:30.399, 12:15:30.442, 12:16:10.461
```

**This does NOT corrupt the decomposition**: the session_end `orders[]` array
carries one row per distinct order id, and `complete_reject_streak` counted
1 -> 2 -> 3 correctly. It corrupts anything that counts the stdout line.
**Any rejection-rate computed from `PMUS_ORDER_REJECTED` rows is inflated 3x.**
Recorded; not fixed here.
