# L5 Maker-Fill Toxicity Analysis

Date: 2026-08-30. Machine-readable source of truth: `l5_fill_toxicity.json`
(regenerate with `python -m src.polymarket_l5.fill_toxicity`). Sample: the
contiguous recent coverage era (2026-08-28T07:45Z → 2026-08-30T21:09Z),
BUY-only fills in resolved windows.

## Question (pre-registered by the V2-replay review)

> Conditional on being filled as maker, what observable state predicts whether
> this particular fill is profitable after adverse selection and rebates?

## Method

- Per-fill settlement attribution `pnl = shares × 1{outcome wins} − dollars`,
  identical to `derived_window_pnl` accounting. **Cross-validation:** summed
  per-fill P&L is −$2,504.34, matching the reconciled V2 causal replay baseline
  (−$250.43417 at 10% scale) to a cent.
- Maker/taker labels from a full-era `takerOnly=true` windowed walk of
  `/trades` (9,000 taker rows), diffed by fill fingerprint: 40,455 maker /
  8,056 taker fills (83.4% maker by count).
- Features computed strictly from pre-fill state (prior fills in window, the
  fill's own price/size/side, clock). Time-ordered 60/40 train/validate split;
  bucket edges fixed before outcomes were examined.
- Rebate overlay is an **empirical flat rate** (observed rebate rows ÷ observed
  volume: maker 0.72¢/$, taker 0.25¢/$), kept separate from trading P&L. The
  actual international program is fee-proportional and unverified; per-fill
  rebate numbers are indicative only.

## Finding 0 — he runs two strategies, split by duration

| Segment | maker fills | taker fills | maker pnl/$ | taker pnl/$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15m windows (BTC) | 40,447 | 1,924 | −0.77¢ | +1.39¢ |
| 5m windows | 8 | 6,132 | (n=8) | −0.08¢ |

**BTC 15m is the maker business; 5m windows are almost purely taker flow.**
The earlier venue-wide "81% maker" figure decomposes into ~95% maker in 15m and
~0.1% maker in 5m. Everything below is therefore effectively a BTC-15m maker
model. His 5m taker book is ~breakeven before taker rebates — cheap hedging /
signal expression, not an income line.

## Finding 1 — aggregate maker economics are a wash with rebates

Maker fills: −$2,816 on $366.9k (−0.77¢/$ adverse selection) vs +$2,642 modeled
maker rebate (+0.72¢/$). Taker fills: +$312 on $146.4k. Net trading −$2,504.
The REWARD pool (~$1,560/day observed) is the actual profit layer.

## Finding 2 — maker-fill toxicity is predictable, and stable out-of-sample

Stable-sign buckets (train AND validate agree), maker fills, pnl per dollar:

| Feature | Benign (stable +) | Toxic (stable −) |
|---|---|---|
| Inventory effect | reduces residual: **+5.2¢ / +1.2¢** | extends residual: −5.9¢ / −3.0¢ |
| Imbalance toward fill side | −0.5..−0.1 (fill on light side): +5.7¢ / +6.1¢ | +0.1..1.0 (fill on heavy side): −5¢..−14¢ |
| Fill price | 0.70–1.00: +2.0¢ / +6.6¢ | **< 0.30: −7.0¢ / −27.8¢** | 
| Same-side streak | first fill of a side: +2.0¢ / +1.2¢ | streak ≥ 3: −2.7¢..−10¢ |
| Burst ≥ 6 same-price | +31¢ / +28¢ (n=181/64, small) | — |

The dominant axis is inventory: getting hit on the side you're already heavy on
is toxic; getting hit on the light side is benign. The single most toxic
population is **low-price maker fills (< 30¢)** — being paid the losing side
late. This matches the window-level result that "cheaper side first" marks
losing windows.

## Finding 3 — an ex-ante quoting policy clears adverse selection

Policies over maker fills (composites of individually stable buckets, chosen on
train; **validate column is the honest number**):

| Policy | kept $ share (val) | val pnl | val pnl/$ | val pnl + modeled rebate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline: all his maker fills | 100% | −$1,501 | −1.04¢ | −$458 |
| A: reduces residual OR imbalance < −0.1 | 46.7% | +$876 | +1.30¢ | +$1,363 |
| **B: A and price ≥ 0.30** | **41.7%** | **+$3,776** | **+6.25¢** | **+$4,211** |
| C: B and streak < 3 | 33.2% | +$2,830 | +5.89¢ | +$3,176 |

Unlike the refuted pair-cost ceiling, this family is **ex-ante implementable**:
it corresponds to which side, price band, and moments you rest quotes at all
(declining to quote has no naked-inventory counterfactual). It is the
quantitative version of the proposed design: passive maker → toxicity filter →
inventory-aware quoting → bounded defensive completion, with his own taker
completions (+1.4¢/$ in 15m) left untouched.

## What these numbers are NOT

- **Not executable P&L.** These are his fills filtered ex post. A clone quoting
  only the light side at ≥30¢ faces a different fill stream: his inventory path
  (which defines "reduces residual") is generated by his full two-sided
  quoting, his queue position is not ours, and a filtered strategy changes its
  own future state. Treat +6¢/$ as an upper bound on the signal, not a
  forecast.
- **Rebate caveat**: flat-rate model; the fee-proportional program will weight
  mid-range prices more heavily, which likely *favors* policy B's kept fills
  (0.30–0.90) but is unverified.
- Two-strategy caveat: the 5m taker book may feed the 15m maker book (signal,
  hedging). Filtering one leg in isolation may not compose.
