# Historical Polymarket fee/cost sources — what we actually possess

Accounting audit, research only. No live orders, no venue writes, no strategy
change, no new backtest. Generated by `scripts/l5_fee_source_inventory.py`
(read-only, disk-only); machine-readable companion:
`analysis/l5/POLYMARKET_FEE_SOURCES.json`.

All numbers describe **another trader's public activity** (Polymarket
`l5zn1bwom8etsk`, proxy wallet `0xb945945d5bcaf7b56834d4da8cdf8f8f94b2db68`).
Nothing here is realized profit for our account.

Every row below is labelled **CHARGED** (an actually debited amount),
**TERM** (a schedule / rate, not a charge) or **UNAVAILABLE**. A term is not a
charge and the two are never mixed. No missing fee is filled with a plausible
number anywhere in this document.

## Headline

The premise that fees are "CONFIRMED on only 1,537 legs" **understates what we
hold, and the gap is closable from disk.** Three things are true:

1. `entryFeesUsdc` is a CHARGED field on 1,537 position rows — but that is a
   single 2026-08-31 snapshot of open/closed positions, not a 1,537-of-N
   sample. It covers **0.20% of the 768,325-fill tape** and is **cohort-skewed
   to 5m** (95% btc/eth-5m), so extrapolating it directly is **not**
   defensible.
2. A second CHARGED source covers the whole tape: the per-fill wedge
   `usdcSize − price × size`, present on **768,325 fills** (2026-03-19 →
   2026-08-31). This is a debited amount, not a model — `usdcSize` is what
   moved. 174,428 fills (22.7%) are fee-bearing, totalling **$53,756.24**.
3. A genuine venue TERM exists on disk and the CHARGED data independently
   reproduces it to ~1e-16: `markets.jsonl.feeSchedule`
   `{rate: 0.07, exponent: 1, rebateRate: 0.2, takerOnly: true}`, i.e.
   **fee = 0.07 × p × (1−p) × shares, on taker fills only.**

So a defensible schedule **does** exist for the crypto up/down corpus, which is
where essentially all of L5's pre-incentive volume sits. The unconfirmed-fee
exposure in the +$20,424 figure can be bounded by that schedule rather than
guessed — see *Bounding the unconfirmed fees*.

### Why `makerBaseFee`/`takerBaseFee` = 1000 is a placeholder, not a 10% fee

Rows #8 and #12 both show `1000`. 1000 bps = 10%, and the verification is
unambiguous:

- **Zero variance.** `1000` appears on 3,528/3,528 crypto markets and
  71/71 sports events, across both `feeType` generations and both
  `feeSchedule` variants. A live effective rate would vary with venue policy;
  this does not.
- **Contradicted by the CHARGED data.** 10% of gross on the 174,428
  fee-bearing fills would be ~$246,235. Observed debited wedge is **$53,756**
  — and that is **218 bps of fee-bearing gross**, 70 bps of all-fill gross.
- **It is a cap/field default, and the real term is `feeSchedule`.** On the
  same records `feeSchedule.rate = 0.07` with `exponent = 1`, and the charged
  data reproduces exactly that (below).
- **`makerBaseFee = takerBaseFee` contradicts `takerOnly: true`** on the same
  record, and the tape shows 77.3% of fills paying exactly zero.

**Conclusion: treat `1000` bps as a schema default/ceiling. It is a TERM with
no demonstrated effective force, and must not be used as a fee rate.** For
PMUS sports specifically there is no usable fee term on disk at all: `1000` is
the same placeholder, `feeSchedule` is absent, and `usdcSize` is absent so no
wedge can be measured. Sports fees are therefore **UNAVAILABLE**, consistent
with `FEE_RECONSTRUCTION.md`'s open question 2.

## The confirmed-fee distribution (`entryFeesUsdc`, n = 1,537)

Single snapshot, 2026-08-31T17:58:53Z. 1,526 rows with fee > 0; 11 exactly 0.
Totals: **$1,185.35** of fee on **$39,003.52** of `grossInitialValue` =
**303.9 bps** aggregate.

|---|---|---|---|
| n | 1,526 | 1,526 | 1,526 |
| min | 0.00128 | 0.000115 | 0.000165 |
| p10 | 0.0686 | 0.002037 | 0.0237 (237 bp) |
| median | 0.47334 | 0.009408 | 0.0491 (**491 bp**) |
| mean | 0.77677 | 0.009617 | 0.0453 (453 bp) |
| p90 | 1.81906 | 0.017052 | 0.0633 (633 bp) |
| max | 10.89507 | 0.017500 | 0.0648 (648 bp) |

(Including the 11 zero rows: per-leg median 0.46956, mean 0.77121, min 0.0.)

Two structural facts fall straight out:

- **`max` fee per contract = 0.017500 exactly** = `0.07 × 0.5 × 0.5`. The
  per-contract fee is hard-capped at the `p = 0.5` peak of the schedule.
- Dividing out the schedule, **implied `rate = fee / (p(1−p) × size)`** has
  median **0.06999**, p25 0.0671, p75 **0.07000** (n = 1,526) — the charged
  amounts reproduce `feeSchedule.rate = 0.07, exponent = 1` to rounding. The
  low tail (p10 = 0.0557) is where a leg's `avgPrice` mixes fee-bearing and
  fee-free fills, so the quoted average price is not the fee-weighted price.

The "effective rate vs notional" is **price-dependent, not constant**: because
fee ∝ p(1−p) and notional ∝ p, fee/notional = 0.07 × (1−p). It runs from ~6.9%
at p = 0.01 to 0.35% at p = 0.95. Quoting a single bp figure for it is
misleading; the rate above (491 bp median) reflects this subset's median
`avgPrice` of 0.207, not a venue constant.

**Whole-tape CHARGED comparison (n = 768,325 fills, 174,428 fee-bearing,
2026-03-19 → 2026-08-31):** fee per contract min 7.0e-5, p10 0.00395, median
0.01312, mean 0.01171, p90 0.01739, max 0.01800; **$53,756.24** total =
**218.3 bps of fee-bearing gross** ($2,462,352). This corroborates
`analysis/l5/fees/FEE_RECONSTRUCTION.md` ($53,537.13 / 173,668 / 761,022) to
within 0.4%; the small difference is overlap between `activity.jsonl` and
`activity_gap.jsonl.gz` that this audit does not de-duplicate, so prefer the
frozen study's figures for P&L work.

## Is the 1,537-leg subset representative? **No — loudly no.**

| dimension | fee > 0 (n = 1,526) | whole tape / fee = 0 rows |
|---|---|---|
| cohort | btc-5m 938, eth-5m 520, eth-15m 62, **btc-15m 6** | tape is **btc-15m-dominated** (693,916 of 761,022 fills in the frozen study) |
| `endDate` range | 2026-04-30 → 2026-08-31 | tape runs 2026-03-19 → 2026-08-31 (March absent) |
| size (contracts) | p10 10.0, median 54.53, mean 85.36, max 2,172 | the 11 fee = 0 rows are **all btc-15m**, median size 15.7 |
| `avgPrice` | p10 0.03, median 0.2071, p90 0.62 | fee = 0 rows median 0.4607 — the opposite side of the p(1−p) curve |
| outcome | 1,505/1,526 `redeemable`, 1,504 with non-zero `realizedPnl` | survivorship: a snapshot of positions still on the books |

Three systematic differences, any one of which breaks naive extrapolation:

1. **Cohort.** 95.6% of fee-bearing rows are 5m markets, which the frozen study
   shows are **100% fee-bearing**. The tape's volume is BTC15m, which is only
   **15.4%** fee-bearing. Scaling this subset's 491 bp median across the tape
   would overstate BTC15m fees by roughly the ratio of those two rates.
2. **Selection on fee-bearing-ness.** All 11 zero-fee rows are BTC15m — i.e.
   maker fills under `takerOnly: true`. The subset is near-purely taker flow.
3. **Snapshot survivorship + truncation.** It is one 2026-08-31 pull of
   positions that still existed, with no March rows, so it is neither a random
   nor a chronological sample.

**Therefore: do not extrapolate the 1,537 legs.** Their value is as an
*independent cross-check that the schedule is the right one* — which they pass
exactly — not as a basis for scaling.

## Bounding the unconfirmed fees

A defensible bound exists, and it does not require estimating anything:

- **Preferred (no bound needed).** The wedge source (#4) is CHARGED and covers
  **100%** of the 768,325 known fills. Any leg in the +$20,424 analysis that
  has a matching `activity` row has its fee as an observed debited amount. The
  honest framing of that figure is therefore not "fees confirmed on 1,537 legs"
  but "fees observed on whatever fraction of its legs join to the activity
  tape" — which should be re-stated before the stress test is scoped.
- **Fallback upper bound, schedule-based.** For any leg that cannot be joined,
  `feeSchedule` gives a hard ceiling with no free parameters:
  **fee ≤ 0.07 × p × (1−p) × size ≤ 0.0175 × size** per contract, with
  **0 as the hard floor** (`takerOnly: true`, and 77.3% of observed fills pay
  exactly 0). The 0.0175/contract cap is confirmed by the charged data's
  observed maximum. That is a true bound in both directions, not a guess.
- **Not bounded.** Gas / on-chain cost (#14) — **UNAVAILABLE**, zero
  observability in this corpus, deliberately not estimated. PMUS sports fees
  (#12) — **UNAVAILABLE**, no schedule and no wedge. Both must be carried as
  named unknowns in any stress test rather than zeroed or filled.
- The 58 `crypto_fees` (v1) markets carry `{rate: 0.25, exponent: 2}` instead,
  and are 1.6% of captured markets (56 btc-15m, 2 btc-5m). Their functional
  form is **not** separately validated here; treat them as a small named
  residual.
- `rebateRate: 0.2` and `makerRebatesFeeShareBps: 10000` are **incentive
  TERMs**, never credited amounts. Per `CLAUDE.md`, they do not net against
  fees until source-confirmed as credited.
