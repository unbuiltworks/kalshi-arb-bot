## 2. Reward qualification from venue evidence (issue #251)

The CFTC filing (PMX-2026-70) could not be read from this environment
(`www.cftc.gov`: proxy 403 and DNS failure). The evidence below is the
venue's own payloads instead (`data/pmus/incentives_2026-11-27/`):
- **The live tennis programme spans contracts:**
  `atp_wta_moneyline_live_20261018` has been active since 18 Oct and covers
  every ATP/WTA match (`$184` live, `$40` day-of, target 19,000, discount
  0.3).
- **So do the current CFB and NBA programmes:** the `cfb_t2` live programme
  covers 43 games. Every contract in a programme carries the same
  `rewardPool`.
- **The 10 Sep data agrees:** up to 2,920 contracts share one
  `programId|period` key.
- **Scoring is per contract:** it is normalised per contract book per
  snapshot.

The pool is therefore **one contract's time period**.

**Change: `PMUS_RN1_REWARD_QUAL_SCOPE=contract_period`.**
- **Pool key:** (market slug, programId, period, start).
- **What counts:** only that contract's own accrual in that period, plus its
  own resting or fresh planned rate, over min(3 h, window left, period left),
  against the unchanged $1.00 minimum.
- **What never counts:** another match, another period, or an expired period.
- **Where it applies:** selection, touch EV, the marginal gate and the
  standing start all use the same test.

**Caveat:** paid rows of $0.11 per sport-day exist in our records, so the
venue may apply the $1 minimum to a wider aggregate. `contract_period` is
the most conservative of the supported readings. It may under-credit
payable rewards, but it never credits an unpayable one.

**Optional caps and minimum volume.**
- **Not seen in any payload:** no executed-notional reward cap or
  minimum-volume field appears in 40,793 recorded periods (10 Sep) or in
  today's active set. Every period carries exactly `programId, programType,
  start, rewardPool, status, discountFactor, targetSize, period, createdAt`.
- **Unknown fields fail closed:** a period carrying any other field is
  treated as not payable (`PMUS_REWARD_UNKNOWN_TERMS`, default
  `fail_closed`).
- **No volume chasing:** nothing trades to meet a volume term. The volume
  programme rewards taker volume, and taker trading is not authorized.

**Rebates:** assumed zero (maker fee 0, rebate 0), unchanged.
