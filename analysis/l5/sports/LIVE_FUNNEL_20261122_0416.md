## The international guard excludes tennis by a naming mismatch, not by absence
The guard looks up `aec-<x>` as international slug `<x>` exactly. That holds for
NBA/NHL/CFB/NFL/CBB. Tennis is listed internationally under a different scheme:

| Polymarket US | International | Intl book |
|---|---|---|
| aec-atp-sebgor-omajas-2026-11-21 | atp-gorzny-jasika-2026-11-21 | 0.48 / 0.49, $33k traded |
| aec-atp-yukmoc-kosogu-2026-11-21 | atp-mochiz-ogura-2026-11-21 | 0.64 / 0.65 |
| aec-atp-thofan-mussik-2026-11-21 | atp-fancutt-sik-2026-11-21 | 0.76 / 0.77 |
| aec-itfwo-saivar-nemkis-2026-11-22 | itf-varana1-kispot1-2026-11-22 | 0.21 / 0.24, thin |
| aec-wcbb-ucirv-sd-2026-11-21 | cwbb-ucirv-sd-2026-11-21 | 0.35 / 0.39, thin |

Same first-named player/team order on both venues in every case checked.
ATP Challenger books on Polymarket US were 1c wide with thousands of contracts at the
top (e.g. 20,192 x 24,093). ITF, UTR, AHL and table tennis are thin or wide on one or
both venues and are correctly excluded.

Truly unlisted internationally (no match by name): table tennis (setka), UTR, AHL,
cricket, NBL, WBBL, smat.

## What would change the count
- Tennis resolver (match by player surnames + date, verify full names and start time)
  plus `atp` on the allowlist: about 3 more markets at this hour; ATP Challenger tennis is
  live most hours of the day.
- `nhl` on the allowlist: 1 more at this hour (mapping already exact).
- The 0.25-0.75 selection band is the largest filter but is a risk choice, not a defect.
