## Soccer is the largest untapped class
- 19 of the 66 live events. Each outcome is its own Yes/No market with its own book:
  Juventus-Venezia showed 0.62/0.63 (45,512 on the ask), draw 0.23/0.24, Venezia 0.14/0.15.
- International lists the same three Yes/No markets (`sea-juv-ven-2026-11-22-juv`,
  `-draw`, `-ven`), quoted within 1c of ours. Serie A uses the same league code on both
  venues; others differ (thai1 -> tha1, tff1 -> tur2, serca -> serc).
- The engine already has a soccer late rule (second half, 70th minute on).
- Needed before it could trade: guard mapping for `atc-` markets (exact slug for matching
  league codes first), tests, and the usual allowlist decision. Lower leagues are thin
  (Serie C: 1-10 contracts at the touch) and would be filtered by depth.
- RN1's 30-day soccer results were mixed (EPL -3.8%, Serie A +4.8%, neither significant).
