# Decision, 2026-11-14: close the 5m straddle, gate the 15m

## 1. The 5m 8c straddle is closed

Every route through it is negative, and they were checked separately:

| | |
|---|---|
| pair rate, shadow n=179 | **7.26%** (breakeven 8.70%) |
| pair rate, pooled with independent tape | 9.69%, CI 7.01..13.23% |
| lone leg wins, shadow | **3.8%** (6/160) |
| lone leg wins, LIVE | **0 of 19** |
| ONE-case EV, held to settlement | **-4.25 c/share** |
| ONE-case EV, best exit (0.20) | **-2.63 c/share** |
| live realized, 2026-11-14 | **-$8.84** over ~20 windows |

Shadow and live AGREE: P(0 wins in 19 | shadow rate 3.8%) = 0.48. The
shadow is not overstating; the strategy is simply unprofitable. Exiting
into a recovery improves a losing position without rescuing it.

Trend as the sample grew, which is the decisive part: t +1.32 at n=55,
+2.03 at n=106, **+1.06 at n=179**. It never cleared, and it is decaying.

**No further live capital on BTC5m.**

## 2. Exit-on-recovery does NOT replicate -- retraction

`straddle_shadow.py` records it as "the only arm in this program to clear
its own search bar (t +2.69 against a 2.04 bar for 8 arms): 31% of legs
that collapse to 8c RECOVER to 12c inside the window, and selling into
that recovery as a maker turns -8c into +4c."

On current data, across seven exit levels:

- **5m:** hold -4.25 c/share; best exit -2.63 c/share. Exiting helps, but
  every variant stays negative.
- **15m:** hold **+10.00** c/share; best exit **+7.20**. Exiting actively
  destroys the edge at every level tested.

The 31%-recover figure survives (33.1% recover to 0.12 on 5m). The
PROFITABILITY claim does not. Holding is optimal wherever holding is
profitable at all.

## 3. The 15m lane is the one live candidate, and the mechanism is known

| | 5m | 15m |
|---|---|---|
| lone leg wins | 3.8% (6/160) | **18.0%** (9/50) |
| ONE-case EV held | -4.25 c/share | **+10.00 c/share** |
| pair rate | 7.26% | 9.1% |
| net per window | +2.30 c, t +1.06 | **+16.73 c, t +2.90** |
| n | 179 | 55 |

The difference in lone-leg survival is **z = 3.42**, not noise. The
mechanism is structural rather than statistical: a leg that collapses to
8c has three times as long to recover inside a 15-minute window. That also
explains why the 15m edge comes mostly from winning lone legs (+500c)
rather than pairs (+420c) -- **it is not a pair strategy at all.**

At n=55 the 15m clears sqrt(2 ln 40) = 2.72, so it survives a 40-arm
multiple-comparisons bar. The 5m never reached t +2.90 at any sample size.

## 4. Why not go live on it now

Because n=55 is exactly where the 5m looked best. The 5m peaked at t +2.03
on 106 windows and fell to +1.06 by 179. Five findings on this branch have
already been retracted after looking good at this sample size: the Q3
volatility spike, lag-2 autocorrelation, the 180s cutoff, DOWN-side
asymmetry, and now exit-on-recovery.

**Nothing on BTC5m reopens on any outcome.**

# RETRACTION, same day: the 15m lone-leg advantage does not exist

The gate above fails. Not on sample size — on condition 4, the number it
was built around.

## What refuted it

The BTC15m session replayed the identical straddle (bid P both sides from
open to 870s, no lookahead, winner used only to settle) on **380 recorded
PMUS BTC15m windows, Nov 3-10** — 7x my sample, and out of my period:

```
8c, through-fill:  15 BOTH, 347 ONE, lone legs won 1.4% (5/347)
net -0.027/window, CI [-0.048, -0.006]
every price 3-15c negative
```

Against my claim of 18.0% (9/50). The two disagree at z ~ 5.0.

## Three checks on my side

1. **Fill rule — not the explanation, though it was the right suspect.**
   My rule fills on a TOUCH at 0.08; theirs requires trading THROUGH.
   Splitting my ONE cases on whether the filled side ever printed below
   0.08: touch-only 2 cases (both won), traded-through 48 (7 won, 14.6%).
   So touch-vs-through moves 18.0% -> 14.6%, nowhere near 1.4%.

2. **My win detection is not broken.** pnl_per_share on 15m ONE cases is
   exactly {-0.08: 41, 0.92: 9}, and the side-comparison and the pnl sign
   disagree on 0 of 50 rows.

3. **It is a TIME CLUSTER.** The 50 ONE cases in window order, W = lone
   leg won:

   ```
   11-13 18:00  ...WWW.........W.W.....WW   7/25 = 28.0%
   11-14 05:00  ................W.W......   2/25 =  8.0%
   ```

   It had already decayed to 8% before the refutation arrived.

## Pooled

```
14 wins / 397 ONE cases = 3.53%        (theirs + mine)
the 5m rate                3.6%
```

**15m == 5m on lone-leg survival.** The z = 3.42 was a 50-window cluster.
The claimed mechanism — "a leg that collapses to 8c has 3x as long to
recover" — was the story told about the cluster, not its cause.

## What this closes

The 15m straddle was the last surviving candidate. With the lone-leg
advantage gone, 15m is 5m at a slower cadence, and MAKER_PRICE_CURVE.md
already closes 5m for maker and taker at every price on both sides.

**The 8c straddle family is closed in both window lengths.**

## The lesson, recorded because it is the expensive one

The gate was the right instrument and would have caught this at n=150 —
condition 4 is exactly the refuted number. But building a gate around 55
fresh windows when a sibling session held 380 recorded ones was the error
before the error. The decay signature was also already visible in my own
data (28% -> 8%) and I reported the blended 18% instead of looking at it
in time order.

Seventh retraction on this branch. The previous six: Q3 volatility spike,
lag-2 autocorrelation, a 180s cutoff, DOWN-side asymmetry,
exit-on-recovery, and "volume cannot be measured on a live window".
