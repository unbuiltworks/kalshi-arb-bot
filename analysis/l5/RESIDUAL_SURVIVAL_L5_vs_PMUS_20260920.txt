READING.
L5 resolved only 10.8% of residual episodes BY TRADING within its market's life;
~90% were carried to settlement. PMUS is slower at short horizons (2.0% vs 8.3%
at 120s) but converges by 600s (6.9% vs 10.8%) and passes L5 by 900s. Neither
median is reached. Both carry ~90% of residual to the end of observation.

=> LONG-LIVED RESIDUAL IS FAITHFUL L5 OPERATION, NOT A PMUS DEFECT.

THE ASYMMETRY THAT REMAINS IS DURATION, NOT BEHAVIOUR. L5's residual was always
extinguished by a 5-15 MINUTE settlement clock. A PMUS live-sports residual is
extinguished by the END OF THE GAME -- hours. The holding BEHAVIOUR transfers;
the EXPOSURE DURATION does not, and that is a venue property, not a policy flaw.
