# L5 mission brief - owner, 22 Nov 2026 (~16:00Z)

Recorded from the owner's instruction in the production-owner session so every session
coordinating through this repository works from the same brief. This file records direction;
it authorizes no trading. Where it differs from older documents on strategy priority, this is
the later owner statement.

## Mission
One continuously operating, profitable prediction-market trading system on the existing L5
infrastructure. Target: $480/day confirmed settled realized net profit, including verified
liquidity rewards and rebates. The target is measured, never assumed.

## Primary strategy
Reward-aware inventory accumulation, not pair arbitrage. Pair completion is incidental.

Priorities:
1. Reward-eligible sports markets - primary development and live validation.
2. Reward-eligible crypto markets (ETH, SOL, XRP, other verified) on existing infrastructure.
3. BTC5m - research only until new evidence shows favourable net economics.

## Controller behaviour
Discover eligible markets continuously; prioritize verified reward programs and attractive
liquidity; quote both sides when justified without requiring balanced inventory; replenish
after fills; adapt quotes and sizes to price, inventory, volatility and rewards; permit
controlled asymmetric inventory; no forced pairing or expensive hedging; let residual positions
settle when their expected economics justify the risk; stop adding when marginal expected value
turns unfavourable; manage exposure per market and across the portfolio.

## Economics
Optimize: settled trading P&L + confirmed liquidity rewards + maker rebates - fees and other
execution costs. No double counting. Modelled rewards, replay fills and quoted liquidity are
never realized earnings. More contracts is not more profit.

## References (behaviour only, profitability unverified)
RN1 wallet, Bonereaper, k9Q2, 0x8dxd, 0x1f0e. Bonereaper's positive platform P&L remains
unreconciled with its negative reconstructed trading P&L.

## Workflow
Autonomous: research, code, regression tests, development branches, reconciliation, disarmed
release preparation. Order: verify production and venue economics -> find the highest-impact
profitability or safety bottleneck -> smallest useful correction -> regression and baseline
parity -> bounded live validation -> explicit authorization -> verify venue-confirmed fills,
rewards and settlements -> improve from real evidence. No repetitive shadow experiments, new
services, duplicate controllers or rebuilds.

## Infrastructure
Reuse this repository, Railway, the existing PMUS engine, reward feeds, reconciliation and the
independent watchdog. One coherent inventory-aware controller with market-specific risk and
reward rules. Sports and crypto changes coordinate through GitHub. No blocking crypto gateway
reads in the sports execution loop.

## Safety and authorization
Preserve capital limits, campaign loss stops, daily loss halts, fail-closed authorization,
exact-order cancellation, durable reconciliation, account ownership protections and
independent watchdog shutdown. A stalled coding or chat session must never prevent an
emergency halt. Explicit owner approval is required for live trading, arming, money-capable
production deployments, taker authorization, capital increases or weakened safety limits.
Strategic direction is never permission to trade.

## Reporting
Changes and regression results; running SHA and trading mode; venue-confirmed fills and
inventory; confirmed rewards and rebates; settled realized trading P&L; total realized net
after rewards and fees; largest blocker; next action and authorization needed; known
infrastructure costs.

Standing rule: every action must advance profitable execution. Optimize reward-adjusted
portfolio economics, not pair count, fill count or gross volume.
