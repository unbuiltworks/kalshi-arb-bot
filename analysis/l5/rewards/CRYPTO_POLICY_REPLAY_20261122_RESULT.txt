Daily-crypto reward lane - policy replay, 22 Nov 2026

Read-only. Public book snapshots replayed through the lane's own quotes()
(src/pmus_crypto_rewards.py) with analysis/l5/rewards/crypto_policy_replay.py.

EVERYTHING BELOW IS MODELLED. No order was placed. There are NO venue-confirmed
crypto fills and NO venue-credited crypto rewards: both remain zero.

Columns
  quotable   share of snapshots in which both sides would rest and score
  usd_day    modelled reward if the sample held all day and the $150 pool is
             split evenly between ETH, SOL and XRP (the split is unpublished)
  fill_qty   MODELLED fills, an upper bound: the best price on our side fell
             below our bid by the next snapshot AND the venue's traded count
             rose; we are given all of that volume up to our size
  mo5m_usd   those modelled fills marked at the mid five minutes later
  worst_leg  worst-case inventory loss: the dearest bid the policy rested,
             filled alone in full and settled at zero

What the two samples say
  * One tick back is quotable 27-85% of the time, depending on the market and
    the hour; the rest of the time the best level alone holds Target Size or
    the second fails Max Spread.
  * Modelled reward, 100 a side, one tick back, three markets:
        00:04-02:01Z tape   1.61 + 4.24 + 0.77 =  $6.62/day
        03:30-03:50Z books  2.38 + 4.07 + 7.97 = $14.42/day
    The spread between two samples of the same day is larger than either
    figure. Neither is an estimate of a day.
  * These markets barely trade: 93 / 8 / 121 contracts in two hours, 315 / 0 /
    0 in twenty minutes. Modelled fills are therefore small (0-32 contracts,
    cents of markout) -- in quiet hours. The samples contain no large move;
    ETH went 0.47 -> 0.30 later the same day and neither sample covers it.
    Fill losses on a move are NOT measured here.
  * Worst-case inventory at 100 a side is $69-75 per market at the current
    lopsided prices (the dear leg costs ~0.70), not the ~$50 of the proposal.
  * Under the hard $30 loss budget: three markets get ~$10 each (~14
    contracts at 0.72, under the 25 minimum -> no quotes); ONE market gets
    ~41 contracts. One tick back that models at roughly $0.3-4.5/day
    depending on market and sample, i.e. on both sides of the venue's $1.00
    payout floor.
  * At the best price the modelled reward is 2-3x higher and in these quiet
    samples the modelled fills are still small. That is the trade-off a longer
    recording has to settle; it is not settled here.

Not measured: a full day (the other ~22 hours), any hour with a real move,
fills against our own orders, how other makers react to our size, whether the
venue credits crypto at all.
