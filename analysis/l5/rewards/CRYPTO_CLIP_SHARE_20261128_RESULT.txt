What it means in dollars (2 contracts a side, one tick back)
  The dollar figure is share x the market's allocation of the $150 pool, and
  that allocation is NOT published. Two bounds, no claim about which holds:
    sum of the three markets' shares      0.41% (sample 1)   0.98% (sample 2)
    if each market had the whole $150     $0.62/day          $1.47/day
    if the pool is split evenly in three  $0.21/day          $0.49/day
  At the best price instead of one tick back:
    sum of shares                         1.61%              3.64%
    whole pool each / even split          $2.42 / $0.81      $5.46 / $1.82
  The venue pays nothing under $1.00. Sports evidence (not crypto) points to
  that minimum applying per programme and period, which would let the three
  markets' accruals add up; it is unverified for crypto.

Worst-case risk at the clip
  One lone leg per market, lost in full: $1.00-1.50; three markets $3.1-4.4.
  That is inside the $30 crypto loss budget and inside the sports per-market
  unpaired cap as shipped ($3).

Is a payout probe economically defensible?
  As income: no. One tick back the modelled programme-period total is
  $0.21-1.47/day against up to $4.4 at risk, and under most denominators it
  does not reach the $1.00 floor, so the likeliest outcome is no credit and
  nothing learned.
  As a purchase of information (does the venue credit crypto at all; how the
  pool is divided): only at the best price, where the modelled total is
  $0.81-5.46/day and clears the floor under either bound in sample 2 and one
  bound in sample 1. Joining the best price is the configuration the lane
  ships OFF (PMUS_CRYPTO_ALLOW_TOUCH) because that is where a quote is picked
  off first. Neither sample contains a real move, so that cost is unmeasured.
  Smallest probe that could answer the question: one programme-day, the three
  daily markets, 2 contracts a side, at the best price, hard loss budget on,
  observation tape on; worst case about $4.5. It is NOT prepared beyond this
  description: no variable is set and no approval document exists. It needs
  its own authorization and, first, the outstanding release gates.

     market's 22:00Z-22:00Z window; the two overlap by ~17 h.

  Neither module is imported by the engine; both are pure and place nothing.
