49s / 168s budget.

### The mechanism, stated precisely

The candidate **works the pair DOWN below $1.00 sequentially**. It deliberately
tolerates a pair cost **above** par after a higher-price EXTEND (measured 1.0137)
and compresses it later (measured 0.9858). The p75 of the *final* achieved pair
cost is 1.0136, i.e. a quarter of matched events never get under par at all and
the strategy still works.

**Therefore the venue requirement is "a sub-$1 pair is SEQUENTIALLY REACHABLE
inside the complement-latency budget", NOT "a simultaneous pair < $1.00".**
Scoring any venue on simultaneous pair cost alone would be the wrong test. It is
reported below only where it establishes a *floor* that forecloses the sequential
path too.

### 3.1 Polymarket crypto up/down — REFERENCE — passes, **circularly**

Source: L5's own fill tape, 763,966 deduped fills, **2026-03-19 → 2026-08-31**.

**Circularity notice, load-bearing:** R1–R10 were *derived from* L5's behaviour on
this venue. Scoring it against them is a provenance and consistency check, **not
independent validation**. It passes the behavioural dimensions by construction.

What is *not* circular, and is the operative finding, is **which dimensions remain
UNAVAILABLE**. We hold L5's own fills but **no book tape**, so **R5 depth, R6
spread and R7 maker adverse selection are unmeasured on the one venue where the
mechanism is demonstrated.** We can show the strategy worked *for L5* here. We
cannot show *our account* could execute it here.

- **Sequential sub-$1: YES, demonstrated — the only venue on disk where it is.**
  Realized VWAP pair cost < $1.00 in **3,518 / 5,100 matched events (69.0%)** and
  ≤0.96 in **2,309**, median **0.9679**, having tolerated p75 **1.0136** en route.
- R10 is L5's *realized notional*, not book depth. Whether our account can take
  the same size without moving the book is **UNAVAILABLE**.

**Binding gap: evidentiary, not microstructural** — R5/R6/R7 need a book tape.

### 3.2 Gemini BTC5M — FAILS, 6 of 10

Sources: `STRUCTURAL_EXPANDED.json` (**61 windows / 24,520 obs**,
2026-08-26 → 2026-09-02); `P0_COMPLEMENT_FORENSIC.md` (**17 windows**);
raw `gemini_bbo_btc05m.jsonl.gz` (**61,497 rows**) and
`gemini_prints_btc05m.jsonl.gz` (**1,962 windows / 15,934 prints**, 6.8 days).

- **R3 is the most absolute failure.** Venue-wide flow is a median **5 prints per
  5-minute window** (p25 3, p90 18) — ~1 print/min — against **≥30
  own-trades/min**. L5 averages **53.8 own fills per event**; the entire venue's
  print count per window is **~11× smaller than L5's own fill count per event**.
  To trade at the candidate's cadence our account would have to *be* roughly 30×
  the whole venue's flow. No policy tuning reaches this.
  *(The median inter-print gap is 1.95s, superficially close to L5's 2.0s — but
  that is burst clustering, p25 0.04s / p75 21.0s, inside a window holding only 5
  prints in total. It is not a sustainable cadence.)*
- **R2 fails independently.** Sequential sub-$1 marketable complement while
  holding a causal T+15 first leg, at 1 Hz with 0.197s measured latency applied:
  **0.0% within 1/5/10/30/60s**, 11.8% within 120s, **47.1% through settlement**.
  First-lock time min 71.0s, **median 174.6s**, max 249.0s. Required completion
  rate for positive EV **71.2%** against **47.1%** available — a **24.1 pp**
  shortfall. Simultaneous pair is **never** below $1.00 (min 1.01, median 1.24,
  n=24,520).
- **R7 fails by 52×:** unrebated maker P&L **−$0.2380/contract** [−0.2831,
  −0.1935] on **10,698 evidenced maker fills / 46 windows**, against a −$0.0046
  tolerance. **0 non-toxic states** in every bin of every pre-fill feature.
- **R5 and R6 PASS.** Depth is explicitly *not* the constraint: sub-$1
  availability is **identical at q1/q5/q10/q25** (8/17 windows, 532.1 open seconds
  at every size). Spread passes — see the correction in §4.1.

**Binding requirement: R3 (trade density) and R2 (complement latency), jointly.**
R3 is unreachable by ~30×; R2 independently forecloses the sequential path.

## 4. Corrections to prior framings — verified, not trusted

### 4.1 The "Gemini BTC5M median spread 0.14" figure is not a spread

`STRUCTURAL_EXPANDED.json`'s own field is **`spread_proxy_q1`, unit `"$ over
1.00"`** — it is the **two-leg pair-cost excess** (`pair_cost − 1.00`), identical
by construction to `pair_cost_q1` shifted by 1. It is **not** a single-leg
top-of-book spread.

Recomputed from the raw tape (`gemini_bbo_btc05m.jsonl.gz`, **61,497 rows**,
2026-08-30 → 2026-09-02): single-leg spread **median 0.02, p10 0.02, p90 0.02**,
mean 0.0283. **R6 therefore PASSES for Gemini BTC5M**, where the brief expected a
failure. The venue still fails on R1/R2/R3/R4/R7/R10 — but not on spread, and the
"bimodal, 41.4% ≥20c" characterisation describes pair-cost excess, not quoting
width.

### 4.2 `gemini_bbo_btc15m.jsonl.gz` carries the UP leg only

Leg census: **UP 23,318 / DOWN 0** of 23,318 rows. This is the *same* defect as
`data/policy_d_port_ext` and it had not been flagged for this file. The BTC15M
tape therefore **cannot speak to pair cost** either, which is why §3.3 reports
R1/R2/R10 as UNMEASURABLE rather than merely unmeasured.

Relatedly, `gemini_prints_15m_tail.jsonl.gz` is **mislabelled**: all 46 symbols in
it are `GEMI-BTC05M…`, not BTC15M.

### 4.3 The `policy_d_port_ext` UP-leg-only claim — VERIFIED, with a correction

Verified for the **Gemini** rows: 46,692 Gemini rows, **45,931 UP-suffixed, 0
DOWN**. The prior finding stands and those rows cannot speak to Gemini pair cost.

**But the file is not only Gemini.** Venue census: **gemini 46,692 / kalshi 43,442
/ meta 17**; kinds: bbo 47,667 / print 40,965 / vol 742 / window 734. The
**2,409 Kalshi bbo rows carry both `yes_bids` and `no_bids`**, so Kalshi pair cost
*is* computable from this file — which is how §3.4's independent corroboration was
obtained. The prior "cannot speak to pair cost" conclusion was correct for Gemini
and **over-broad for the file as a whole**.

## 5. Verdict

### Does any venue pass?

**No independent venue passes.**

Exactly one venue-category passes the mechanism requirements — **Polymarket crypto
up/down**, the venue L5 actually operated in — and it passes **circularly**,
because R1–R10 were derived from L5's behaviour on it. Even there, **R5 (depth),
R6 (spread) and R7 (maker adverse selection) are UNAVAILABLE** because no book
tape for that venue is on disk. We can show the strategy worked *for L5* there.
We cannot show *our account* can execute it there.

The structural asymmetry is the finding:

> **Every venue where we can measure the book fails the mechanism. The one venue
> where the mechanism is demonstrated is the one where we cannot measure the
> book.**

### What this implies for next measurement (not a strategy change)

The scoring points at one measurement, not at a deployment: **a two-leg (UP+DOWN)
book and print recorder on Gemini BTC15M**. It is the only alternative habitat
that passes every dimension its data can currently measure (900s windows, 26×
depth headroom, 0.02 spread) and whose remaining gaps are all evidentiary. A
read-only recorder would convert 7 UNAVAILABLE scores into measurements and
decide the venue either way.

Secondarily, the reference venue's three UNAVAILABLE dimensions (depth, spread,
maker adverse selection) are the gap between "L5 did this" and "we could do
this", and closing them also needs a book tape rather than a new hypothesis.

No deployment, parameter or live-progression change is proposed or implied by this
document.
