9. WORST-CASE SETTLEMENT DEFICIT (default disabled; fail-closed when set)
   tests/test_l5_worst_case_deficit.py
   deficit = cost - min(up, dn), maximised over which outstanding orders
   fill. Below zero the book pays out on BOTH settlement outcomes. No order
   is exempt: a completing order is priced exactly like an opening one.

10. TWO-SIDED ONE-WINDOW PROBE (experiment-gated; inert by default)
    tests/test_l5_two_sided_probe.py
    Generates exactly two post-only intents or a terminal refusal. Owns no
    transport, ledger, cancellation or gate. Refuses a pair whose edge does
    not clear the worst-case fee on both legs, which is what resting AT the
    touch on a one-tick book always is.

11. TRADE-THROUGH OBSERVER (additive, read-only, not a fill model)
    tests/test_l5_trade_through_observer.py
    Records never_reached / touched / traded_through per leg with
    timestamps, queue-ahead bands and the cancellation deadline. A touch is
    never a fill and trade-through is never a confirmed fill. The existing
    two-sided collector's API and outputs are untouched.

12. TWO-SIDED PROBE WIRING (experiment-gated; inert by default)
    tests/test_l5_two_sided_wiring.py
    Both legs reach the transport through the ordinary path with
    participateDontInitiate true, the PAIR is gated before either leg is
    claimed, each leg holds its own one-shot durable claim, and a refusal
    anywhere submits nothing rather than falling back to the L5 intent.

13. PROBE TERMINAL DEADLINE BOUNDARIES (real runner method)
    tests/test_l5_ts_deadline_boundaries.py
    cancel_cutoff takes the window END and slug_start gives the START.
    Passing the start gave start-180: a deadline expired before the window
    opened. Boundaries at +0/+300/+719/+720/+721s, cancellation by exact
    venue id proved by re-read, and a caller audit for the whole class.

14. PATIENT OPPOSITE-SIDE COMPLETION (economics-driven; crossing is a
    SEPARATE authorization)
    tests/test_l5_patient_completion.py
    tests/test_l5_completion_wiring.py
    completion_decision takes NO clock, so the deadline can never be the
    reason to cross. It crosses only when the worst of the two settlement
    outcomes strictly improves by MORE than the taker fee that bought the
    improvement, and only inside the cash headroom and worst-case ceiling
    already in force. Worst-case improvement ALONE is measured to be nearly
    vacuous once a book is one-sided -- it passes at every price up to 0.95
    -- which is why the fee bar exists. Unattested cost basis, a stale book,
    absent depth and unreadable exposure each refuse rather than default.

15. DEPTH COMPARISON (read-only; refuses to conclude without a sample)
    tests/test_l5_depth_comparison.py
    1 tick vs 2 ticks decided on realized edge TIMES the measured both-leg
    fill rate, from actual order and fill records. A deeper quote always
    shows a larger QUOTED edge, so the quoted edge is never used as a
    substitute; below the sample thresholds no rate and no verdict is
    reported, and a missing commission withholds the realized edge rather
    than reading as zero.
